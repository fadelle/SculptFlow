Integrations

Every channel your leads already use

Clinics rarely get inquiries through one channel. SculptFlow brings them together instead of asking your team to work across three separate apps.

WhatsApp

Manage consultation inquiries and follow-up on the channel most clinics already get the most messages on.

Instagram

Bring DMs and comment-driven inquiries into the same inbox as everything else, instead of a separate app.

Facebook Messenger

Keep Facebook page conversations flowing into the same shared inbox as WhatsApp and Instagram.

More channels can be added over time — the inbox is built to hold conversations from wherever your leads reach out.
Unified inbox

One inbox for conversations from every channel

Instead of switching between apps, your team works from a single shared inbox — with the full conversation history attached to every lead.

  • Shared inbox across WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger
  • Complete conversation history, in one timeline
  • Staff replies and AI replies both visible, clearly labeled
  • Message status, so nothing sits unanswered by accident

AI + human takeover

Automation assists. Your staff stay in control.

Automation can qualify leads, answer common questions and follow up — but any team member can take over a conversation at any moment, and hand it back to automation later.

  • See every conversation, whether AI or staff-led
  • Reply manually whenever you want to step in
  • Take over from AI, and hand a conversation back later
  • Nothing sends on the clinic's behalf without this being visible
